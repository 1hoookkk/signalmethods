# TRENCH — 60-second demo, shot list

One take if possible, screen + audio captured together. The pitch is the
loop: play → morph → resample → the machine eats its own output.

**Prep (before recording):**
- FL project: one drum loop channel (punchy, dry), one dark pad channel.
- TRENCH on the drum bus, default body (Morph LP X), rooms closed, MIX 100.
- FL mixer hidden; plugin window centered, 100% — the small face IS the shot.

| # | sec | shot | action | the point |
|---|-----|------|--------|-----------|
| 1 | 0–6 | face at rest | loop plays dry 2 bars, then drop TRENCH in | small box, instant identity |
| 2 | 6–16 | MORPH close | slow sweep 0→100 and back, curve dancing | every position is a real filter |
| 3 | 16–24 | BODY menu | hover-audition 3 bodies live, land on one | filters are cartridges |
| 4 | 24–32 | GAIN room | open GAIN, SLAM up hard, GRIT to taste | density and balls |
| 5 | 32–40 | MOVEMENT | MOTION → a phrase; hands off; wheel plays itself | the machine performs |
| 6 | 40–52 | SOURCE | play a fill, press RESAMPLE, wave freezes; click USE AS INPUT; morph the frozen take | the loop: it eats its own output |
| 7 | 52–58 | drag out | drag the wave into the FL channel rack, trigger it | the take is a real file |
| 8 | 58–60 | face at rest | hands off, quiet plate, logo beat | small thing, big punch |

Audio rule: no talking, the sound is the argument. Text overlays max three
words per shot ("EVERY POINT PLAYS", "IT EATS ITSELF", "DRAG IT OUT").
