# TRENCH — quickstart

A morphing filter you play like an instrument, in a small box that packs a
punch. One wheel does the talking.

## The wheel
**MORPH** melts one filter into another. Every point of the travel is a real
filter — park anywhere, or perform it. **Q** pushes the body's second axis:
sharper, stranger, yours. Drag anywhere on a wheel to jump there; Shift for
fine; double-click to reset; scroll always works.

## BODY
The bar up top picks the filter you're holding. Hover the menu to audition
bodies live before you commit. **NO FILTER** takes the filter out of the path
and leaves SLAM and MIX playable — the clean-drive trick.

## KEY
TRENCH listens to your track and suggests its key. Click the suggestion and
the filter locks musically to the song.

## The rooms
The small bar under Q opens one room at a time — the plate stays quiet until
you ask:
- **GAIN** — PREAMP in, GRIT (the filter's own distortion), SLAM out. SLAM is
  the drive that gives a sound density and balls; MIX blends the whole wet
  path against the dry.
- **MOVEMENT** — MOTION plays the wheel for you: synced rates, factory
  phrases, RISE. AMOUNT sets how far it travels.
- **SOURCE** — the machine's memory. The glass always shows your last hit,
  exactly as a RESAMPLE press would keep it.

## Resample (the loop)
Play something. Press **RESAMPLE** — the last hit you played is kept, exactly
as it sounded. Your track is untouched. From there:
- **drag the wave** straight into your DAW — it's a real file, the exact take;
- **USE AS INPUT** — run the take back through the machine and keep morphing
  it. Amber means the take is feeding. **▸ LIVE** hands your track back;
  double-click RESAMPLE steps a generation back.
Nothing you play gets kept quietly. If there was nothing to keep, the glass
says so.
