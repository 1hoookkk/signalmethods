# THE STAGE LAW — permanent authoring authority (Tyson verdict 2026-08-03)

Derived from the decoded P2K bodies, probed at 44.1 kHz through the packed
runtime path. This is the binding grammar for authoring every TRENCH body,
per filter type. It supersedes the earlier placement-pattern notes.

**Canonical source (found 2026-08-03):** the raw 240-byte bins in
`df2-workstation/ref/presets/` (see its README and `P2K_MANIFEST.json`,
50 filters with provenance). JSON skins are derived and lossy — never
authority. `ref/p2k_variants` holds 200 raw variants; **variant 0 is the
runtime-verified main bank.** The 15 plotted bodies match their canonical
bins 15/15 by SHA-256.

The one-sentence law:

> **Build a broad spectral frame, move several resonant lanes as a
> formation, hold or counter-move one or two zeros to preserve the
> silhouette, and use Q either to sharpen that frame or reveal a second
> authored pose.**

## Motion grammar (measured, all bodies)

- Six pole/zero lanes form ONE composite shape. A section may look extreme
  alone; later sections cancel or reinforce it. Judge only the whole.
- MORPH moves a formation, not a knob: median pole travel 1.16 octaves;
  57% of poles travel more than an octave.
- Most pole/zero pairs travel together (married pairs). ~28% counter-move —
  the counter-movers preserve a cavity, notch, tilt, or high-frequency
  boundary while the main feature travels.
- **Stage number is correspondence, not frequency rank.** Crossings and
  lane hand-offs are intentional. Never fit corners independently and
  frequency-sort; the lane keeps its identity across all four corners
  (S6 stays S6: one coherent interpolated journey).
- Q is NOT consistently "more resonance": it raises pole radius in 77% of
  comparisons but ALSO moves pole frequency in 66% (38% by over an octave).
  A Q100 face may legitimately be a different authored pose.

## Spectral territories (neighbourhoods, never exact tunings)

No chromatic/note tuning exists: across 680 decoded frequencies, distance
to the nearest semitone is statistically random. Do not snap to notes.

- Pole activity: ~2, 4, 5 kHz (12–13 of 15 bodies)
- Whole-filter peaks: 4–5 kHz and ~10 kHz (12 of 15)
- Upper "teeth": ~12.5–16 kHz (11 of 15)
- Common canyons: 2.5–3.15 kHz and 8–10 kHz

## The air-cap anchor (corrected against the full 50-filter bank)

A zero at **20,277.1 Hz, radius 1.0**, always in S6: the high-edge /
air-cap counterweight. **Common, not universal** — present in 26 of 50
bodies across the complete bank (it was universal only within the first
15 studied). Treat it as a standard tool, not a requirement.

## THE FAMILY LAW — the strongest full-bank pattern

E-mu built families by **preserving and handing off authored frames**,
changing selected lanes and levels — not by generating every filter
independently, and not by merely turning resonance up. Measured:

- Millennium and Meaty Gizmo share their complete Q0 plane (12 exact full
  rows), then diverge at Q100.
- Eeh to Aah M100/Q0 hands five of six geometries into Ubu Orator M0/Q0.
- Fuzzi Face and Cruz Pusher both use the five-stage fixed 10,887.9 Hz
  zero wall.
- Boland Bass and Lucifer's Q share seven section geometries.
- Klub Klassik feeds four geometries into Acid Ravage; Acid Ravage and
  Tooth Comb share three exact Q100 geometries.

Authoring implication: a proven TRENCH frame is a legitimate parent —
derive siblings by re-voicing selected lanes and levels, and chain preset
endpoints into longer journeys. Reuse OUR frames only; the bespoke rule
(no copied E-mu rows) stands.

## Full-bank motion statistics (exotic 000–032 set)

- Median MORPH pole travel 1.21 octaves; 62% of pole lanes travel more
  than an octave.
- Q moves pole frequency by a median 0.57 octaves; 39% move over an
  octave. Q raises pole radius in 69% of comparisons — and LOWERS it in
  29%. Q is a posture, not a resonance knob.
- Still no chromatic/note-frequency grid anywhere in the bank.

| Comb (Dream Weava, Klang Kling, Tooth Comb) | Near-linear ladders (Dream Weava M0: 4.10/7.11/9.52/11.75/14.08/16.55 kHz, ~2.49 kHz spacing; Klang Kling ~2.3–2.6 kHz spacing) with shift-register hand-offs: stages land on neighbouring stages' former frequencies. |
| Freak (Lucifer's Q, Ear Bender, Freak Shifta) | Deliberate extreme crossings (90 Hz → 10.65 kHz against 19.96 kHz → 404 Hz) or broad high-to-low collapse; most lanes travel over an octave. |

Continuity is a legitimate device: Eeh to Aah M100/Q0 and Ubu Orator M0/Q0

to each other.

## Bespoke rule

All 360 complete section rows across the corpus are unique. The shared
character is the motion grammar, never copied rows. TRENCH bodies are
authored bespoke inside this grammar with OUR endpoints (measured curves,
mined selections, simulated circuits) — Tyson picks the endpoints, always.

## Enforcement

- Fitters must preserve lane identity across corners (pole-lock / joint
  fits / behavior-searched pairing; never frequency sort).
- Certification and judgment happen on the probed whole cascade only.
- The cascade inspector (`tools/inspect_body.py`) is the standard view for
  checking a body against this law: chain, build-up, corners, lanes.
