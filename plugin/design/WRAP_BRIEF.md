# WRAP BRIEF — how the functions get their final skin
2026-07-31, for the next session. Brainstorm, not law. Verdicts pending.

## What today taught (the design rulings that held)

- **Chrome lives in the photographed object.** Painted overlays failed three
  times on the knobs; baking the tick-ring into the filmstrip solved it in
  one. Any future indicator starts as a bake question, not a paint question.
- **Same material promises the same gesture.** The knob cap on a button said
  "turn me." Rotation-averaging gave us pressable metal from the same render.
  Material family = gesture family, always.
- **State speaks from its control, not from the glass.** The GEN tag and the
  note letters on the peaks "ruined the UI." The glass is the sound and the
  law — never a status bar. KEY's lock lives in the KEY box; GEN lives on
  the button. Full stop.
- **Nothing new at rest.** Every signal added must be state-gated. The rest
  face is the product photo.

## The elegant-wrap ideas to brainstorm

1. **One temperature per voice, finished.** Coral = the sound. Teal = your
   hands. Amber = the source (RESAMPLE). Nothing else ever gets a colour.
   Audit every pixel against this — anything grey/white that signals state
   is a candidate for deletion or one of the three voices.
2. **The glass recreation as the unifier.** When we rebuild the bitmap
   (GPT-art pipeline + check_faceplate.py), bake the grid, the well shadows,
   and the corner radii to match the plate's real lighting. One light source
   across plate, wells, knobs, button — that's what "one machine" feels like.
3. **The tab as RESAMPLE's room.** The button sits in the reserved tab. If
   it still floats: engrave the tab's purpose into the plate art itself
   (recessed circle in the RECREATED plate bitmap, not runtime paint) so the
   home exists even before the button lands there.
4. **Retire, don't add.** Candidates to question next session: the FX dim
   word when nothing is on (earns its rest-state pixels?), the "DISTORTION
   TUCKED AWAY" announce copy (harness caught it looking silly — is the
   announce line itself pulling weight?), the MOD/DIST/FX word states.
5. **Onboarding carries the teaching burden.** Every explanation we were
   tempted to draw (what GEN means, what KEY lock does) becomes one tour
   sentence instead of face pixels. One new step: FX. Maybe one: RESAMPLE.
