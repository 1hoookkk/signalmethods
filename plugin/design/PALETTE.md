# THE FACE PALETTE — petrol-citron

The warm taupe panel remains the locked 60. The visualiser is a dark coloured
instrument window like the reference, but shifted away from E-mu cyan into a
deep petrol green. One pale citron signal is shared by the response trace and
the hottest transmitted wheel light.

| Role | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 60 panel | `#9E917C` | locked faceplate artwork; never recoloured |
| 30 glass | `#1E5149` | dyed petrol-green visualiser field |
| grid / support | `#3C7569` | restrained graticule and secondary telemetry |
| 10 signal | `#D8F09D` | response trace and wheel hot core |
| lamp falloff | `#7FA82D` | transmitted wheel light below the hot core |
| aperture edge | `#0D221E` | bezel return and deepest glass edge |

## Visual grammar

- The reference supplies hierarchy, not colours: dark coloured glass, a quiet
  grid, a pale response trace, and the same light family inside the wheels.
- The trace is a flat 1.1 px line. No bloom, aura, fill or modern OLED glow.
- The graticule belongs beneath the glass and remains subordinate to the trace.
- The wheels keep the locked saucer body and measured broad transmission field.
- The plate, white readouts and MIX ladder stay outside the palette pass.
- Apply with `python tools/apply_theme.py petrol-citron`.
