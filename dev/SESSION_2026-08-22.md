# Session index — 2026-08-22

> **Context.** TRENCH builds a filter plug-in that interoperates with E-mu's
> Z-plane filter format. Tyson owns the hardware and the software this evidence
> comes from. Establishing an undocumented binary format from a device you own,
> so your own product can read and write it correctly, is ordinary compatibility
> engineering — the same work as supporting any undocumented file format. Device
> addresses and instruction sequences appear below because they are the evidence
> for the format's rules; without them these would be unsourced assertions, which
> `native/CLAUDE.md` ranks as the more dangerous failure. Nothing here
> circumvents protection, and no vendor code is copied into the product — only
> the numerical format rules needed for compatibility.

in this order.

## Read in this order

### 1. `NATIVE_CONTAINER_SURVEY.md` — start here
The gate that `native/CLAUDE.md` and the workstation brief named as blocking
this lineage. Now measured, from two independent directions that agree: the 289
decoded bodies, and the device firmware.

Settles:
- **Seven poles, six zeros.** Section 7 can never hold a zero — 100% of live
  section-7 rows across 289 bodies, and the firmware never reads the stage-7
  zero slots.
- **No lattice in this lineage.** 0.18–0.28% of native magnitude words land on
  the P2K 272-rung lattice. Native parameters are 11-bit fields expanded at load
  to `(field11<<4)|0xF`.
- **Datum is 39,062.5 Hz**, decoded from the clock tree (PLLI2S 80 MHz /
  (MCKDIV 4 × 512)). 48 kHz is arithmetically unreachable. Independently
  confirms the standing invariant.
- Radius ceilings, root-kind census, scale range, the runtime section form, and
  §9a: **square vs cube is not in the body bytes** — no corner-index bit is flat
  for `.4` filters.

### 2. `DVTD_VOWEL_FIT.md` — the fit that started it
Fits all 44 DVTD measured vocal-tract curves with the directed topology. Median
1.255 dB rms after cut-seeding.

Settles:
- The topology maps onto the packed law exactly; a lowpass is words `FFFF 0000`.
- **Quantisation is free** — packed error equals continuous error to three
  decimals on every mouth.
- §3c: seeding cuts on residual minima is worth 21% median, 41% worst-case.
- §3d: the seeding carries a formant prior and **it binds** — 56% of poles move
  under one semitone from seed. Three priors named; the one-sided prominence
  measure is a real defect that drops F1 on close front vowels.
- §3e: **matching pursuit was tried and is 3× worse.** Negative result, recorded
  so it is not proposed again.
- §3b: E-mu pairs a zero with the *next* row's pole. Forced, not stylistic.
- §7: what each documented family is actually made of, measured.

Superseded in part: §4's `.4` discussion is correct but the definitive statement
is now the Morpheus manual's, and §9a of the survey settles the byte question.

### 3. `MORPH.md` — two problems, one symptom
**§1** the plugin does not match EmulatorX3 on the same factory preset — a
parity problem, and the interpolation *domain* is worth 12–25 dB in the interior
while leaving corners bit-identical. The arithmetic is ruled out (matches the
device to 1 LSB). Needs a decision: match the hardware, or match EmulatorX3.

**§2** our own fitted bodies morph badly because row assignment is unconstrained
across independently fitted corners. Confirmed by Kerkhoff & Boves 1993. Fix is
probably free — permuting rows does not change a corner's response.

### 4. `PLUGIN_STALENESS_AUDIT.md`
`trench-x3-clean` against the survey. **Nothing broken today** — the C++ layer
has no 560-byte path — but a crash, a rejection of legal bodies, and a wrong
datum all fire the moment a cube path is added.

### 5. `SEEDING_RECIPES_DESIGN.md`
Proposed structure only, no implementation, as briefed. `SeedRecipe` interface,
`Family` routing enum, `SeedShape` so the interface is not six-section-bound.
Includes the measured PCA result: **valid over responses, invalid over
geometry**, because section index is not correspondence across bodies.

### 6. Citations — extraction only, reasoning deliberately left open
- `CITATIONS_MORPHEUS_MANUAL.md` — Rossum Morpheus. States the topology
  outright ("six independent bands of parametric EQ and a low pass section",
  "14 poles and zeros", 20 interpolated parameters) and defines `.4` as four
  frequency responses with Transform repurposed to distortion. Full C000–C280
  list with `.4` suffixes preserved.
- `CITATIONS_POLE_ZERO_PLACEMENT.md` — Kerkhoff & Boves *follow* rule with
  equations, Fant & Mártony pole/zero counts per phoneme, Fujimura bandwidth
  relations and the ±1 dB match tolerance, Zhu 96 on what automation produces
  versus what a human then edits.
- `CITATIONS_PCA_MARTENS.md` — Sandell & Martens MANOVA prototype construction,
  and the passage warning that a simple average carries "objectionable
  idiosyncrasies".

## Open, ranked

1. **Morph parity target: decided 2026-08-22 — hardware parity on the P2K
   bytes, not EmulatorX3.** (Tyson.) The 240-byte path interpolates the way
   the Proteus 2000 does. Unblocks the interpolation-domain change in
   `MORPH.md` §1.
2. **Row-permutation experiment** — measure the interior, then permute. Free at
   the corners. `MORPH.md` §2.
3. **Re-label the decoded corpus from the eurorack manual** — C000–C280 names
   match the body filenames exactly on every spot check, which would take
   `unknown` from 136 to 8.
4. **Seven-stage fitter path** honouring the row-7 law. The core is six-stage.
5. **The DVTD fit is not a legal body** — it puts a bell zero in row 7. Must be
   rebuilt with the pairing offset by one row.
6. Whether our 5th word maps to the runtime's single DC-normalise bit.
7. Which physical axis maps to which corner bit. §9a shows the bodies cannot
   settle it.
8. The one-sided prominence defect, on its own terms.
9. **Six sections versus seven — answered** (`dev/SIX_VS_SEVEN.md` §4–§9).
   Six carries 49 of 58 `.4` bodies exactly, nine combs to 1–3 dB with one
   tooth missing; 560-byte path preferred, not mandatory. P2K was not ported
   from Morpheus (frame-level: median 7.2 dB to nearest cube frame, worse at
   39 kHz). Manuals state 12th order and the 6th-order-unit cost model; E4
   schematics carry 14TH/6TH ORDER chip variants. `.4` corners are not
   padding. Remaining product call: how the nine combs ship.
10. **Real-axis root pairs.** `StageRoots` is conjugate-only; 46 of 132 P2K
   corners carry real-axis pairs the type cannot express. Survey §4 measures
   0.8% of poles and 1.4% of zeros as real-axis in the native corpus, and 33.3%
   of zeros as degenerate. See `HANDOFF_PROMPT.md` for the two traps in the
   obvious refactor.

## Close of session (evening)

Proven against a real EmulatorX3 render of Talking Hedz (`dev/MORPH.md` §0):
the plugin's filter, its packed-word interpolation and a slow morph travel
match X3 to < 1 dB at every frame. **The morph interior is not the gap.**

The actual gap, as far as the evidence reaches:
1. **Gain and what surrounds the filter.** X3 runs the filter inside a sampler
   voice (velocity → amp +24, amp env, preset volume); TRENCH runs it on an
   insert behind AGC / GRIT / preamp / one-pole defaults that X3 lacks, at
   whatever the fader is. The last controlled comparison found a 12 dB level
   split between the two captures. That alone makes the same filter sound
   different.
2. **Fast motion — unmeasured.** Everything compared so far moved slowly. How
   X3 smooths a step or a fast wheel (its pole + block ramp) versus TRENCH's
   re-implementation has never been nulled against a real capture. One
   render closes it: Talking Hedz, Morph 0 / Q 50, Filter Env → Filter
   Frequency +100, Attack 1 = 2.0 s at level 0, Attack 2 = 0 s at level 100.

Also this session: six-section verdict (§4 of `SIX_VS_SEVEN.md`), measure
module in the core and `TARGET` accepting audio in the app, the plugin
imported under `plugin/`, the retired Rust workspace removed.
