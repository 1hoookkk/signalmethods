The exact bit-manipulation and floating-point expansion for the 16-bit piecewise-logarithmic words (4-bit exponent, 12-bit mantissa) is defined as follows:  Decoded word value $d \in [0.0, 1.0]$:$$\text{Let } u = w + 1, \quad E = u \gg 12, \quad M = u \ \& \ 0\text{x0FFF}$$  $$\text{If } E = 0: \quad d = \frac{M}{4096} \cdot 2^{-15}$$  $$\text{If } E > 0: \quad d = \left(\frac{M + 4096}{8192}\right) \cdot 2^{E - 15}$$  Direct Form II monic biquad coefficients ($1 + a_1 z^{-1} + a_2 z^{-2}$ denominator, $b_0 + b_1 z^{-1} + b_2 z^{-2}$ numerator) evaluate to:
  $$b_0 = 4d_4$$  $$b_1 = (4d_0 + d_1 - 2) \cdot 4d_4$$  $$b_2 = (1 - d_1) \cdot 4d_4$$  $$a_1 = 4d_2 + d_3 - 2$$  $$a_2 = 1 - d_3$$  Identity pass-through (parked zeros): $d_0 = 0.25$ (0xDFFF), $d_1 = 1.0$ (0xFFFF), $d_4 = 0.25$ ($b_0 = 1.0$).  2. The Word Coupling Invariants (The Critical DSP Bug Fix)Word 2 / Word 3 Coupling: Because $d_3 = 1 - R^2$ and $4d_2 = (2 - 2R\cos\theta) - (1 - R^2)$, pole radius $R$ and angle $\theta$ are coupled across both words.  The Defect: Editing radius via Word 3 alone shifts the resonant center frequency by up to 15.2 cents.  The Law: Any radius/bandwidth edit must re-encode both Word 2 and Word 3 with $\theta$ held constant.  Zero Coupling: The same rule applies to zeros; editing zero depth or bandwidth requires re-encoding both Word 0 and Word 1.
