Real - time interpolation may be per formed simultaneously along three separate axes . Various embodiments of the present technology include receiving , in real - time via a digital filter module , at least one acoustic signal and one or more control input signals indicative of one or more corresponding interpolator values . The methods and systems may further include determining one or more filter coefficients based on a linear interpolation in an encoded space of a plurality of fixed frequency responses , generating a filter from the one or more determined filter coefficients , and applying the generated filter to the at least one received acoustic signal .
