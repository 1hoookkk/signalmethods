# Peterson and Barney 1952, vowel formant averages

Peterson, G. E. and Barney, H. L. (1952), "Control methods used in a study of the vowels",
JASA 24, 175-184. `pb52_means.csv` holds the average F1, F2 and F3 in Hz for the ten vowels
by speaker group (33 men, 28 women, 15 children), the table reproduced in most phonetics
texts. The values were transcribed on 2026-09-07 from the commonly reproduced averages
table, not from the paper itself, which is not in evidence: verify against the paper before
quoting a number as law. Bandwidths are not given by the study; HEADSPACE builds these frames
with `vowelWords`, the same fixed widths it uses for Klatt and Hillenbrand.
