Jean-Bernard -

RAULT Jean-Bernard CNET/DIH/REN wrote:

> *       objective analysis of natural sounds to derive low level descriptors

> *       mapping of low level descriptors to high level descriptors

> *       coupling of low parameters with the synthesis methods to get

> synthetic sounds as perceptually close as possible to given natural sounds

> *       coupling of high parameters with the synthesis methods to get

> synthetic sounds as perceptually close as possible to desired sounds.

You will be very happy to find the papers that Andrew Horner et al. have

published, especially in the JAES (Journal of the Audio Engineering Society) on

the analysis techniques that he has developed.  He has published specific

parameter sets for CMUSIC, which should translate into MP4-SA quite easily, for

numerous real instruments, including strings, bells, and others.  Andrew's stuff

is excellent, and extremely efficient.

Just look through the AES journal index for Author: Horner for the past 5 to 7

volumes.
