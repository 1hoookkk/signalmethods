## ir_library/ — instrument-body & object impulse responses
Source: https://github.com/itsmusician/IR-Library (author "Conner", itsmusician).
License: MIT (copy saved as ir_library/License.md). Selected files downloaded from
raw.githubusercontent.com/itsmusician/IR-Library/main/Instruments/...

- cymbals/ — China cymbal contact IRs (resonant + damped) and SDC sweep capture
- violin/ — violin body IRs (resonant + dampened) and sweep
- ukulele/ — concert + soprano ukulele body IRs (close mic mono, contact mic)
- piano_upright/ — Winter Upright piano soundboard/string resonance IRs
  (open lid default/sustain/bass-sustain, sympathetic resonance)
- misc/ — kalimba body resonance, steel pan sweep, glockenspiel sweep

## modal_tables/ — published measured modal data
- cymbal_bell_gong_modes.md — church bell partial series, C5 handbell modes,
  40 cm cymbal low modes, tam-tam/gong behavior, grand piano soundboard modes.
  Sources: Rossing "Science of Percussion Instruments" (2000); Rossing & Perrin
  Applied Acoustics 20 (1987); Rossing & Peterson Percussive Notes 19(3) (1982);
  Touze & Chaigne Acta Acustica 86 (2000); Fletcher Rep. Prog. Phys. 62 (1999);
  Suzuki JASA 80(6) (1986); Giordano JASA 103(4) (1998).
  Transcribed numeric data (facts, not copyrightable); citations are the record.
